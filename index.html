How prop firm funding works

Prove your skill. Trade their capital.

A proprietary (prop) trading firm funds traders who can show consistent, disciplined results. You take an evaluation, the exam, and if you pass you trade a funded account instead of risking your own savings. We coach you through the whole process.

Trading your own money

  • Your savings are what is on the line
  • Small accounts limit how much you can trade
  • Every loss comes out of your own pocket
  • Pressure makes disciplined trading harder

Trading a funded account

  • You trade the firm's capital, not your savings
  • A larger account to work with once funded
  • Clear rules that build discipline and risk control
  • A structured path from beginner to funded trader

  1. 1Learn the model

  2. 2Prepare with a coach

    We help you build your trading plan and practise it against the exam rules before you sit it.

  4. 4Get funded

    Pass and you trade a funded account, following the same rules you proved you can keep.

Key terms, in plain English

Evaluation (the exam)
A trading test with a profit target and strict risk rules. Passing it unlocks a funded account.
Funded account
An account backed by the firm's capital, which you trade under its rules once you have passed.
Profit target
The amount you need to make during the exam to show your strategy works.
Drawdown limit
The maximum loss allowed. Break it and the attempt ends, which is why risk control comes first.
