FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What if I have no money to trade with?

You don't need a large account of your own. A prop (proprietary trading) firm lends skilled traders its capital. You take the firm's evaluation, the exam, which has a profit target and strict risk rules. We coach you through it, from your trading plan to your execution. If you pass, you trade a funded account backed by the firm's money instead of your savings, under the same risk rules you proved you can keep. Passing is not guaranteed and depends on your discipline and results, and exam rules and terms are set by the funding programme. Trading involves risk.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. The Course starts from the foundations and builds to full execution, step by step.

Is this legit?

It's a structured programme built around one strategy, with live lectures and a student community. We never promise income. The wins above are individual results, not a guarantee of yours.

Will this make me profitable?

We teach you a process, and we never promise income. Trading carries substantial risk. Results depend on your practice, discipline and risk control.
